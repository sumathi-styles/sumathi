Privacy Policy
Last Updated: September 23, 2026

At Sumathi's Styles, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to keep it safe when you use our application and services, including shopping, custom orders and classes.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Sumathi's Styles may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Account login information

Order and Delivery Information

  • Delivery address
  • PIN code
  • Contact phone number
  • Order details
  • Product and quantity information

Custom Order Information

If you use our Custom Order feature, you may voluntarily provide information necessary to process your request, such as:

  • Custom design requirements
  • Dress or tailoring preferences
  • Measurements or fitting information
  • Reference images uploaded by you
  • Voice notes or other information submitted with a custom order

Classes

If you register for or enquire about classes offered through Sumathi's Styles, we may collect information required for registration, communication and class management.

Reviews and Feedback

If you submit reviews, ratings, feedback or other content, the information you choose to submit may be stored and displayed as part of the relevant service.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process and manage orders.
  • Arrange delivery of purchased products.
  • Process and respond to custom orders.
  • Manage class registrations and enquiries.
  • Communicate with you regarding orders and services.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our products, services and application.
  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the application.

3. Payments - Razorpay

Sumathi's Styles uses Razorpay as a third-party payment gateway to process applicable payments securely.

Razorpay Payment Information

Payment information required to complete a transaction may be processed by Razorpay in accordance with Razorpay's applicable privacy policy, security practices and terms.

Sumathi's Styles does not intend to store your complete debit card number, credit card number, UPI PIN or banking credentials on its own servers.

Payment-related information may be handled directly by Razorpay or other authorized payment service providers involved in the transaction.

4. Firebase and Cloud Services

Sumathi's Styles may use Google Firebase and related cloud services to support application functionality such as account management, database storage, authentication, file storage, notifications and application operations.

Information stored through these services is handled according to the applicable service provider's policies and security practices.

5. Uploaded Images and Files

When you voluntarily upload images, reference materials, measurements, voice notes or other files for a custom order, those files may be stored and accessed for the purpose of processing your request.

Please do not upload sensitive personal information that is not required for your order or service request.

6. Notifications and Communications

We may send notifications or other communications relating to your account, orders, custom orders, classes, services, important updates or customer support.

You may be able to manage notification permissions through your device settings.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, including with:

  • Service providers supporting our application.
  • Razorpay or other payment service providers when you make a payment.
  • Delivery or logistics providers when required to deliver an order.
  • Cloud and technology service providers.
  • Government or legal authorities when legally required.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, complete transactions, maintain business records, resolve disputes and comply with applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely disposed of where reasonably practicable.

10. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Sumathi's Styles account and associated personal information by contacting us.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting or legitimate business purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

Sumathi's Styles is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, removed.

12. Third-Party Services and Links

The application may contain links or integrations to third-party services. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party services that we do not control.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, application features, legal requirements or privacy practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or a data deletion request, please contact Sumathi's Styles through the official contact details provided in the application or website.
